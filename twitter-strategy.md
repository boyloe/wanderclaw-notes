## 📌 Content Pillars

### 1. Building in Public (~30%)
Failsafe progress, shipped features, wins and failures. Honest dev journey.
- *Example:* "Spent 3 hours debugging a Prisma adapter issue so you don't have to. Here's what broke."
- *Example:* "Just shipped exponential backoff to our alert queue. Reduced noise by 40%."

### 2. Learning AI by Doing (~25%)
Agentic AI concepts, tools tested, opinions forming as you go. Beginner-honest, dev-literate.
- *Example:* "Two months into agents. Here's what actually works vs. the hype."
- *Example:* "Built a browser automation agent today. It broke immediately. Here's why."

### 3. RV Reality (~25%)
Solar installs, things that break, internet setups, full-time travel logistics. Practical + honest.
- *Example:* "Installed 400W solar this weekend. Our office now runs on sunlight."
- *Example:* "My office lost power mid-deploy because I left the inverter on overnight. Cool."
- *Example:* "Full-time RV internet: Starlink vs. Verizon vs. campground WiFi. Thoughts?"

### 4. Crossover Posts (~20%)
The magic intersection. Ship from campsites. Code while life happens. Makes tech posts human.
- *Example:* "Working remotely from a campground in Utah. The view doesn't suck."
- *Example:* "Two cats, one laptop, solar power, and a deadline. This is my office now."

## 📅 Cadence

- **3-4 posts/week** — quality over volume
- **1 thread every 2 weeks** — threads = growth engine
- **Daily engagement** — 10 min to reply/comment on posts in your niche (AI, full-stack, dev tools, RV tech)

## ✍️ Voice Guidelines

- **No promotion.** Facts, not claims. "Building Failsafe" not "shipping world-class products."
- **Self-aware.** Acknowledge the chaos, the breakdowns, the learning curve.
- **Specific.** Real numbers, real tools, real problems — not generic advice.
- **Humble.** You're figuring it out. That's the interesting part.
