## Important Colorado-Specific Flags

- **Altitude:** Both properties are ~9,300–9,700 ft — verify modular manufacturer builds for high altitude (structural, HVAC, snow load)
- **Snow load:** Roof specs must meet CO mountain requirements (~50–60 psf ground snow load)
- **Water rights:** Colorado prior appropriation — confirm well permit eligibility before buying
- **Frost depth:** ~4 ft at this elevation — foundation and utilities must be buried accordingly
- **Modular vs manufactured:** Lake County zoning may distinguish — confirm "modular" (built to IRC code) is allowed on RR zoning
- **Perc test:** Required before septic permit — do this as a contingency before closing
- **Covenants:** EE Hill has covenants — request and review before making offer

## Septic Notes (Both Properties)

There is **no public sewer** in the Twin Lakes area. Both properties require an engineered septic system:

1. **Perc test** (~$500–1,500) — tests soil drainage; must pass before permit issued
2. **Septic system design** by licensed engineer (~$1,500–3,000)
3. **Lake County permit** (~$500–1,000)
4. **Installation:**
   - Standard gravity system: $15,000–25,000
   - Engineered/mound system (rocky/sloped terrain): $30,000–50,000
   - Alternative/aerobic system (if perc fails): $40,000–60,000
5. **Setback requirements:** 100 ft from well, 10 ft from property lines — important on smaller lots like Spruce Dr (1.38 ac)

**On Spruce Dr (1.38 acres):** Setback requirements are tight. A 100 ft well-to-septic separation on 1.38 acres is manageable but needs careful siting. Verify with Lake County before committing.

## RV / Travel Trailer Living on Land During Construction

### The Strategy
Buy land → get building permit → live in trailer while you build. Very common approach in rural CO.

### Lake County Rules (from 2025 Land Development Code)

**Section 5.4 — Temporary Residence Permit:**
- You CAN live in an RV/travel trailer as a temporary residence while building
- **Requires:** Active building permit for a permanent home obtained FIRST
- **Duration:** 12 months per permit, up to 2 renewals = **36 months (3 years) max**
- Applies to districts: AF, AR, UR, RM, RC, B and R

**Section 5.2.25 — Camping on Private Property:**
- Camping is allowed by right in RUR, AF, AR, UR, B, RC, and BC districts
- No explicit time limit stated
- Conditions:
  - ✅ Max one camping unit per parcel
  - ✅ Unit cannot be permanently affixed to the ground (must stay on wheels)
  - ✅ Must have current registration and be road-worthy
  - ✅ Sewage must be properly disposed (permitted septic OR haul-out)
  - ✅ Address must be visible from road
  - ✅ Bear-resistant trash containers recommended
  - ✅ No burning within 150 ft of water body

### ⚠️ Critical Issue — RUR Zoning at Reva Land
- Reva Land is zoned **RUR (Residential Rural)**
- The formal "Temporary Residence while Building" provision (Sec 5.4) lists **AF, AR, UR, RM, RC, B, R** — **RUR is NOT on this list**
- "Camping on Private Property" IS allowed by right in RUR with no time limit
- **Gray area:** Is living in your trailer "camping" or "residence"? Lake County may treat them differently.

### Action Required
**Call Lake County Land Use before buying:**
- 📧 landuse@lakecountyco.gov
- 📞 (719) 486-2875
- Ask: *"Is a temporary residence permit available for an RV on RUR-zoned land once a building permit is obtained? Or is it limited to camping only?"*
- Also ask: *"Does the E.E. Hill subdivision have any covenants restricting RV/trailer occupancy during construction?"*

## Lake County Zoning Districts Explained

| Code | Full Name | What It Means |
|---|---|---|
| **RUR** | Residential Rural | Very low-density rural land. Primarily agricultural/open space with limited residential. Single-family homes may require conditional use permit. Reva Land and many Twin Lakes parcels are this. |
| **AF** | Agricultural Forestry | Agricultural/forestry use with some residential. More rural than AR. Allows single-family homes. |
| **AR** | Agricultural Residential | Ag land transitioning toward residential. Single-family homes permitted. More flexible than RUR. |
| **UR** | Urban Residential | Standard residential near towns/subdivisions. Single-family, duplexes permitted. Pan-Ark Estates (Spruce Dr) is likely this or AR. |
| **RM** | Residential Multi-Family | Higher density residential — apartments, condos, townhomes. |
| **RC** | Rural Commercial | Commercial uses in rural areas — shops, services, small businesses. |
| **B** | Business | General commercial/business district. |
| **R** | Residential | Standard residential in incorporated areas. |
| **BC** | Business Commercial | Higher intensity commercial. |
| **CI** | Commercial Industrial | Mixed commercial/light industrial. |
| **IM** | Industrial/Mining | Heavy industrial or mining operations. Common in Lake County due to mining history. |

### Why This Matters for Your Parcel
- **RUR** is the most restrictive for residential use — it's designed for open space, not development
- Many RUR parcels require a **Special Use Permit** or **Rezoning** to build a single-family home
- Before buying any RUR parcel, confirm: *"Is a single-family dwelling a permitted or conditional use on this specific parcel?"*
- The Reva Land listing says "Residential Rural" zoning — this is the **RR/RUR** designation
- If the parcel was previously platted for residential use in the E.E. Hill subdivision, a home may be permitted by right — but **verify with the county**
