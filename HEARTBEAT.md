# HEARTBEAT.md — Twitter Posting Schedule

Check these items during heartbeat:

## Daily (5 min check)
- **Twitter engagement:** Spend 5-10 min replying/liking posts in your niche (AI, dev tools, RV tech, full-stack)
- Have you engaged today? Yes/No

## 3x Per Week (Mon/Wed/Fri)
- **Post ready?** Check if you have a post queued (from `twitter-drafts.md`)
- **Ship it** if yes
- Track what you posted in `twitter-log.md`

## Weekly Review (Sunday)
- **Performance check:** Which posts got engagement? Tell me the winners.
- I'll update `voice-guide.md` with patterns.

## Notes

- Don't force posts if you have nothing to say
- Engagement (replies/likes) > posting cadence
- Track posts in `twitter-log.md` so we can analyze
