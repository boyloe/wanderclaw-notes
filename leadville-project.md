### Key Cost Assumptions

**Land (CO, rural, mountain area):**
- Baseline: $3,500–$6,000/acre
- Leadville area (higher elevation, colder): tends toward $4,000–$5,500/acre
- 20–50 acres = $100k–$250k depending on location/zoning

**Modular Homes (3bed/1.5bath, factory-built):**
- Base price (factory): $120k–$180k
- Delivery + setup: $10k–$20k
- Upgraded finishes: can push to $200k–$250k
- **Realistic range for this spec: $150k–$220k**

**Foundation:**
- Concrete pad (2,000–2,500 sq ft): $10k–$20k
- Excavation + grading: $5k–$10k
- Piers/posts if slope: +$5k–$10k
- **Total: $20k–$35k depending on site**

**Utilities (assuming no existing infrastructure):**
- Well drilling (400–600 ft typical): $5k–$8k
- Septic system (3bed = ~1,500 gal/day capacity): $8k–$15k
- Electric service (runs from pole to house): $3k–$8k
- Gas (if propane backup): $2k–$5k
- Driveway (0.25 mile gravel): $2k–$4k
- **Total: $20k–$40k**

**Permits & Professional:**
- County land use permit: $500–$2k
- Well permit: $200–$500
- Septic permit: $200–$500
- Electrical inspection: $200–$400
- Surveyor (boundary + utility map): $1,500–$3,000
- Engineer (foundation design for modular): $1,500–$2,500
- **Total: $5k–$10k**

## Search Filters (Based on Financial Modeling)

To keep projects within budget, use these filters when searching:

### Land Search Parameters
- **Price per acre target:** $3,500–$5,500/acre (typical mountain CO range)
- **Total land budget:** $100k–$150k (leaves $250k–$300k for home + build)
- **Acreage range:** 5–30 acres (flexible — smaller plot = lower maintenance, larger = more privacy)
- **Elevation:** 8,000–10,500 ft (Leadville is 10,152 ft; Buena Vista ~7,965 ft)
- **Must-haves:**
  - Road access (drivable — critical for construction logistics)
  - No deed restrictions blocking modular homes
  - Water source available (well-friendly geology)
  - Buildable slope (ideally <15% grade for foundation work)
  - Utilities feasible (well, septic, electric within reasonable distance)

### Modular Home Search Parameters
- **Budget range:** $150k–$200k (factory price + delivery + setup)
- **Bedrooms:** 3
- **Bathrooms:** 1.5+
- **Square footage:** 1,200–1,600 sq ft (typical for this size)
- **Key features:**
  - High R-value insulation (mountain winters are harsh — aim for R-30+ walls, R-60+ roof)
  - Vented crawlspace or slab-on-grade foundation compatibility
  - Standard utility hookup points (water, sewer, electric, gas)
  - Metal roof or asphalt shingles rated for heavy snow load

### Area Notes

**Leadville (Lake County, 10,152 ft):**
- Highest incorporated city in US
- Ultra-alpine climate (cold, short growing season)
- Historic mining area — some claims/deed restrictions possible
- Good for investors seeking value; less appealing for year-round living (harsh winters)
- Road access critical (mountain passes close in winter)

**Buena Vista (Chaffee County, 7,965 ft):**
- More moderate climate than Leadville (~1,200 ft lower elevation)
- Better for year-round living
- Arkansas River access (irrigation potential, recreational value)
- Close to Collegiate Peaks — popular outdoor area
- Better infrastructure + amenities than Leadville area

**50-Mile Radius Coverage:**
- Lake County: Leadville, Turquoise Lake, Twin Lakes area
- Chaffee County: Buena Vista, Salida, Poncha Springs
- Fremont County: Cañon City area (lower elevation, warmer — but farther south)

## Zoning & Legal

**Lake County Zoning:**
- Land Development Code available at lakecountyco.gov
- Modular homes explicitly allowed under county code (Chapter 5: Use Regulations mentions "manufactured housing")
- Contact: Cece Williams, Planning Admin, (719) 839-2255 or cwilliams@co.lake.co.us
- Minimum lot size varies by zone (typically 5–20 acres in rural areas)

**Chaffee County Zoning:**
- Mixed-Use Rural zoning available (new code effective 1/1/2025)
- Modular/factory-built homes treated same as site-built for zoning purposes
- Contact local planning department for specific parcel zoning

**Colorado State:**
- Modular homes regulated under CRS 24-32-3305
- Factory-built structures subject to technical advisory committee recommendations
- No state-level ban on modular homes (fully legal + equal to site-built)
