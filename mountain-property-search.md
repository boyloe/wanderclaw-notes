## Target Towns — Ranked by Priority

### 🔴 Primary Targets (Start Here)

1. **Salida, Colorado** ⭐ RECOMMENDED
   - Elevation: 7,000 ft
   - Vibe: River town + outdoor hub, walkable downtown
   - Price Range: $250–350k for 3 bed homes
   - Inventory: 89 homes under $400k (68+ with 3+ bd)
   - Why: Alpine feel, good market depth, proven community
   - Tradeoff: None — solid all-around choice

2. **Alpine, Arizona** ⭐ HIDDEN GEM
   - Elevation: 8,000 ft (White Mountains)
   - Vibe: Escape desert heat, mountain town + ranching
   - Price Range: $200–350k (best value!)
   - Inventory: 35 homes under $400k (9 with 3+ bd)
   - Why: Cheapest option, mountain feel, winter-mild
   - Tradeoff: Smaller market, less established than Salida

3. **Pagosa Springs, Colorado** ⭐ HIGH INVENTORY
   - Elevation: 7,000 ft
   - Vibe: Hot springs resort town, 4-season destination
   - Price Range: $250–400k
   - Inventory: 308 homes under $400k (90+ with 3+ bd) — MASSIVE!
   - Why: Most choices, hot springs amenity, resort infrastructure
   - Tradeoff: More touristy, potentially more competition

4. **Moab, Utah** (Secondary consideration)
   - Elevation: 4,000 ft (lower, milder winters)
   - Vibe: Desert-mountain blend, world-class recreation
   - Price Range: $250–400k
   - Inventory: 61 homes under $400k (60+ with 3+ bd)
   - Why: Unique aesthetic, emerging market, outdoor culture
   - Tradeoff: Market heating up (prices rising), lower elevation

### 🟠 Secondary Markets (Backup Options)

- **Victor, Idaho** — Off-grid, Teton views, $300–400k, 16 listings u$400k
- **Livingston, Montana** — Yellowstone gateway, $200–350k, 24 listings
- **Pinedale, Wyoming** — Pristine wilderness, $250–350k, median $399,900 (tight!)
- **Torrey, Utah** — Capitol Reef access, $300–400k, tiny market (~15 listings)

## Key Reminders

### Budget
- Total: $400k
- Down: $50k
- Mortgage @ 7%, 20yr on $350k: ~$1,800/mo P&I
- Est. total monthly (P&I + utilities + tax + insurance): $2,400–2,700

### Mountain-Specific Considerations
- **Elevation:** 7,000–8,000 ft is sweet spot (alpine feel, acclimatization easier)
- **Winter:** Road access, snow maintenance, utility costs
- **Internet:** Check fiber availability vs. satellite
- **Wildfire risk:** Research county hazard maps
- **Water:** Well vs. municipal, water rights
- **Isolation:** Balance solitude with access to town amenities

### Market Intelligence
- **Salida:** Stable, good depth, slower market = room to negotiate
- **Alpine:** Emerging, low competition, great value — less inventory
- **Pagosa:** Hot market, many choices, resort economy, tourism potential
- **Moab:** Market tightening, prices rising, trendy but still affordable
